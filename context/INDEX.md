## Where things stand

Brand Deal Matcher is a website for small/homegrown Indian brands: a brand
describes its product and gets ranked growing Instagram creators (1K–100K) with a
match score and a reason. Code lives in `~/Documents/brand deal matcher` and is
pushed to the private repo https://github.com/aarnavp25-sys/brand-deal-macher
(`main`, latest 490a342 plus this context commit). Design docs:
`docs/product-design.md` (product, flows, business) and
`docs/data-and-matching.md` (verified Apify facts, pipeline, taxonomy, matching,
load gotchas, LLM/AWS notes; supersedes older Apify/scoring parts).

The prototype is all Python stdlib and runs locally. `pipeline/` scrapes with
Apify under hard spend caps (`apify_run.py`), filters/enriches/classifies with
keywords (`process.py`, `keyword-v1`) into 16 categories and 42 product types
(`taxonomy.py`, with per-round search-term dicts `ROUND5_TERMS`…`ROUND8_TERMS`),
and loads Supabase (`yqaducvspzrqokcqwlga`, ap-south-1, free; RLS on, no
policies; raw JSON in Storage `raw-scrapes`). After eight scraping rounds on
three free Apify accounts, Supabase holds **1,010 creators, 863 usable**, 12,515
posts and 5,037 product links; every category has 31–92 usable creators across
Pune, Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Lucknow, Bangalore, Hyderabad and more. 115
creators have follower snapshots on two dates (2026-11-16, 2026-11-17) from the
Profile Scraper — the start of growth tracking. `admin/` (port 8787) is the local
admin dashboard; `demo/` (port 8788) is the brand-facing demo (brief →
"We understood" → ~20 mixed Specialist/Wider/Potential creators with score
breakdown and templated explanation). Keys live only in `admin/.env` (gitignored).

What is weak: classification is keyword-based, so product matches are noisy
(e.g. `rider_girls_pune` for men's jewelry via "chain"); creator gender and
content quality are empty; brief parsing and explanations are keyword/template.
All wait for an LLM: no Anthropic key yet; AWS has $100 credits (Free plan,
until 2027-05-16) but Bedrock access to Claude was never tried, and the user
postponed AWS on 2026-11-17.

How S-004 ended: all scraping finished and loaded, everything committed and
pushed. Apify left: account 1 $0.30, account 2 $0.43, account 3 $0.65; all three
reset 2026-12-15/16. Tokens for the three Apify accounts and the Supabase
`sbp_…` token were pasted in chat and must be revoked/regenerated by the user;
the copies sat only in the session scratchpad.
